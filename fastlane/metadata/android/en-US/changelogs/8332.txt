Security release — hardened backup encryption.

Update: PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 raised to 600,000 iterations with a random per-backup salt
Fix: Authenticate encrypted backups with AES-GCM; tampered/truncated archives are rejected on restore
Fix: Fresh nonce per encrypted archive (no AES-GCM nonce reuse)
Update: Store the settings vault in an Android Keystore-backed AES-GCM store (auto-migrated)
Note: Encrypted backups use a new on-disk format and are not restorable by earlier releases
